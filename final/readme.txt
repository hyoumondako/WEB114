I decided to make a meme generator with dog-themed images. I love dogs and enjoy seeing silly dog pictures on social media so I wanted to pick five to showcase here. 

This was a great learning experience in the types of event listeners and how they work, like change and input. 

I realize that adding too many characters in the text box will cause the text on the meme to get cut off, but the math involved in fixing it was really complex. Instead, I decided to reduce the overall font size.
